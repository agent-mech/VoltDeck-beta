Fault isolation guide
Error 10 — dash ↔ ESC comms failure ▶
⚠ Disconnect the battery before any resistance test and let the capacitors drain. You'll be probing ~44–54 V rails — work carefully.
1 · Line continuity & splices
Expect < 0.5 Ω per line. Silent / open loop → a wire has torn, usually inside the folding joint.
2 · Voltage-rail audit
Logic rail below 3.0 V → the ESC's on-board regulator is thermally damaged; the node drops out.
3 · Bus transceiver & impedance
Both lines should read a similar, non-zero impedance. A dead 0 Ω short to ground = a blown transceiver (ESD) in node 0x20 or 0x21 — replace it.
4 · Sniffer isolation
A. You see 5A A5 … 21 20 … out but no reply from node 20 → dash is fine, the ESC is dead or boot-looping.
B. You see telemetry from source 5A A5 … 20 21 … but the panel is dark → the dashboard MCU or its flash config has failed.

Operating guide

        

          
For hardware you own. Buttons below are the HUD's
            Executive Action Core hotkeys; fault codes map to the dictionary in Diagnostics → Protocol reference.


          

            
1

            

              
Connect the transport

              
On Protocol Target Configuration, pick Wireless BLE RF (phone/PC — binds to a
                "Segway"/"Ninebot" device) or Wired CAN Bus (an MCP2515 bridge at 250 kbps into the stem wiring),
                then press Initialize Connection Engine. The setup page closes and the HUD opens.

            

          


          

            
2

            

              
Authenticate · EXECUTE_SYSTEM_AUTHENTICATE

              
The ESC ignores commands until the comms cycle starts. This fires the wake/handshake
                to the motor controller. On open or developer firmware it's accepted immediately and the link goes active.

            

          


          

            
3

            

              
Release the wheel lock · FORCE_ESC_CORE_UNLOCK

              
Writes 0x00 to lock register 0x70. You'll hear a click from
                the rear wheel, the electronic brake releases, and the throttle goes live — the scooter now rolls freely.

            

          


          

            
4

            

              
Hold it open · COMMENCE_KEEP_ALIVE_STREAM

              
Commercial ESCs re-lock on a watchdog timer if they stop hearing from the app. This
                pumps a keep-alive every ~100 ms (set the interval on the HUD). Leave it running and the scooter stays
                usable without cutting out mid-ride.

            

          


          

            
5

            

              
Park & re-lock · CEASE_MOTOR_EXECUTION

              
Stops the keep-alive and sends a lock command to register 0x70. The strong
                electronic brake re-engages instantly so the scooter can't be rolled away.

            

          


          

            
6

            

              
Monitor live values & faults

              
Watch LINE_VELOCITY_KMH update as you spin the rear wheel. The
                FAULT card reads OK when healthy; on a hardware fault it turns red with the code —
                e.g. E55 = battery cover open — telling you exactly which physical switch to fix for full power.
                Diagnostics maps the code to its full description, and POLL_SMART_BMS_TELEMETRY checks pack health.
